## 2. TRUTH-IN-ML DISCLOSURES

### 2.1 The Reality of "Multi-Signer" Synthetic Data
- `alphabet_dataset_multisigner.json` contains 4,000 landmark configurations categorized under participant IDs `Participant_1` through `Participant_8`.
- **Truth-in-ML Finding**: These participant IDs do **NOT** represent biological human signers. They represent distinct anthropometric parameter configurations generated by `build_alphabet_dataset.py`, incorporating parametric hand scale factors ($0.08 - 0.18$), bone ratio variations ($\pm 15\%$), palm aspect ratios, and motor noise distributions ($\sigma = 0.01 - 0.03$).
- **Strict Policy**: In all TITAN publications and benchmarks, these 4,000 samples are classified strictly as *synthetic anatomical profiles*, never as real human participants.

### 2.2 Trajectory Dataset Forensic Reality
- `dynamic_jz_trajectories.json` contains 600 base motion episodes (200 NEUTRAL, 200 J, 200 Z) attributed to `Signer_1` through `Signer_5`.
- **Truth-in-ML Finding**: Zero trajectory episodes are empirical human video captures. All 600 episodes were procedurally synthesized using differential kinematic equations modeling 2D/3D trajectory curvature, stroke acceleration profiles, and Gaussian sensor jitter.
- The 5 signer IDs represent distinct parametric motor velocity and curvature profiles ($0.35\ \text{s} - 1.40\ \text{s}$ duration range).

### 2.3 Curated Empirical Human Cohort
- `real_human_evaluation_dataset.json` contains 42 curated, ground-truth annotated frames captured from live front-facing mobile webcams of **6 actual biological human signers**:
  - `Signer_1`: 7 samples (Training cohort)
  - `Signer_2`: 8 samples (Training cohort)
  - `Signer_3`: 7 samples (Held-out test cohort)
  - `Signer_4`: 7 samples (Held-out test cohort)
  - `Signer_5`: 6 samples (Held-out test cohort)
  - `Signer_6`: 7 samples (Held-out test cohort)
- **Held-Out Test Cohort**: Signers 3, 4, 5, and 6 (27 samples across letters A, B, C, D, E, F, L, O, R, U, V, Y) are **strictly isolated** and never exposed to training routines.

## 3. PARTITIONING & LEAKAGE VERIFICATION

To prevent data leakage, cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprinting was executed across all coordinate arrays:
1. **Coordinate Hash Collisions**: 0 instances of identical landmark configurations between training and testing partitions.
2. **Signer Grouping**: For the empirical human dataset, Leave-One-Group-Out (LOGO) and held-out cohort partitioning strictly isolate entire human recording sessions.
3. **Augmentation Discipline**: All geometric perturbations (rotation, scale, noise) are generated strictly at train time; zero augmented duplicates exist in the test partitions.

## 4. DATASET QUALITY LIMITATIONS & MITIGATIONS

1. **Empirical Class Coverage**:
   - The real-human dataset contains 42 samples across 12 alphabet classes. Letters G, H, I, K, M, N, P, Q, S, T, W, X lack empirical human video records in this repository.
   - *Mitigation*: TITAN explicitly discloses this limitation. Synthetic test performance is reported separately from empirical human held-out performance.
2. **Dynamic Signs Real-Human Data Absence**:
   - Real-world dynamic J and Z human video clips are not yet collected in this offline repository.
   - *Mitigation*: Dynamic evaluation is benchmarked against procedural differential equations and physical browser automated stress tests, pending field recording.
